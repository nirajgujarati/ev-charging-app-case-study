# 07. Monetization

These models were identified in research as ways the category earns. None is tested for this concept. No revenue figures are claimed.

| Model | How it works | Fit with this concept |
|---|---|---|
| Session commission | Percentage of each charging session paid through the app | Strong, since payment is the check-in |
| UPI and payment margin | Margin on payments | Depends on payment partner terms (not verified) |
| Subscription plans | Paid tier for users | Possible, for example booking priority or estimates |
| Host model | Owners list chargers and earn, app takes a share | Matches self-listing (as Kazam and Bolt.Earth do) |
| B2B SaaS | Charging-management software for operators | Natural extension if owners adopt the app |
| Data licensing and fleet contracts | Sell status and usage data, serve fleets | Later stage, needs scale |

## Notes
- No-show fees on booking can be both a trust tool and a small revenue line (design item).
- Pricing, take rates and unit economics are **not public / not researched** here and are left for future work.
