## Market value forecasts
- One estimate puts the market at **USD 3.7B by 2030**.
- Another estimate puts it at **USD 1.9B**.
- The two forecasts **do not agree**. Treat both as forecasts, not facts. The disagreement is itself useful: it shows how uncertain the market definition is.

## Reading the numbers
- Vehicles are growing faster than the number of public stations can comfortably serve, which is why queues and reliability show up in user complaints (see `04-user-pain-points.md`).
- Policy tailwind exists (PM E-DRIVE), but policy money mostly funds hardware, not software trust or data quality. That is where an app can differ.

## Caveat
The ~72% CAGR compares two data points that are 7 months apart in how they are labelled (total stations vs public stations in different periods). It is a rough indicator, not a clean time series.
