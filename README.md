# EV Charging in India: Case Study

**Author:** Niraj Gujarati
**Type:** Product research and solution design (no code shipped; see "Status" below)
**Research date:** 25 October 2026

## One-line summary
Indian EV owners carry three or four charging apps because none of them is trusted. This case study documents the market, the competitors, 14 user-reported pain points, and a product concept (self-listed stations, payment as check-in, car-based time estimates, slot booking) designed to answer them.

## Status and honesty note
- This is a **research and design** case study. The product concept is **designed, not built and not validated with users**.
- Market numbers come from the public sources listed in each file. Where two forecasts disagree, both are shown.
- Competitor figures (chargers, funding, downloads) are **company or press claims** at the time of research, not audited numbers.
- Pain points are quotes or paraphrases of public posts and app-store reviews. Links are in `04-user-pain-points.md`.
- Anything not public is marked "not public" or "estimate".
